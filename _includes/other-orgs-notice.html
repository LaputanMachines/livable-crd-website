{%- comment -%} Band for what other organizations are doing in the same election: a questionnaire on a subject ours leaves out, a campaign to get people voting at all. Content comes from _data/other-orgs.yml, so a placeholder becomes a live link by filling in one `url` rather than by editing markup. The band never waits for a URL. Every entry renders; `url` only decides what the last row of a card offers: blank url - a "not published yet" pill, an optional timing line, and a link to the organization rather than to the thing itself. set url - a button through to it, labelled by `action_label`. Two entries sit side by side on a wide screen and stack on a phone, so neither one reads as a footnote under the other. Renders nothing when the data file has no entries.
