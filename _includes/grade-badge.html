Used by the scorecard matrix, the per-candidate topic rows and the per-question rows inside a published subject, so every grade on the site is rendered by this file and they cannot drift. Five states. The last four all look "empty" to a reader and mean different things, so each carries its own glyph and its own border treatment — never colour alone, because the leaflet prints without backgrounds. a letter the grade the coalition assigned N/A graded, and the answer is that the question does not apply to this candidate (ROL-05 asks about a previous term in office) hourglass the candidate returned the questionnaire and this topic has not been published yet. Passed in as state="review" by the caller, which is the only one that knows whether a reply came back. speech the candidate answered and nobody grades this topic, so there is something to read and there will never be a letter. Passed in as state="answers". A clockwise circular arrow replaced it and read as Reload, because that is what that arrow means everywhere else on the web — an instruction to click, on a chip that is not a control.
