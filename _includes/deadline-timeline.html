{%- comment -%} Homepage project timeline: every entry in _data/deadlines.yml in date order, marked done / in progress / up next / upcoming so a reader can see what stage the project is at without reading the dates and doing the arithmetic themselves. It shows the coalition's own milestones alongside the candidate cut-off: the point here is the shape of the whole schedule, and a cut-off makes more sense when you can see the questionnaire being built ahead of it. The two stay distinguishable, because the cut-off carries a visible "Candidate deadline" tag and the milestones do not. Uses the `short`/`brief` copy from the data file. The milestones share the page width here, and the full `label`/`detail` wording does not survive being squeezed into a column of it; that version runs in the /faq/#deadlines panel. An entry carrying `until` is a period rather than a moment - the stretch the schedule is inside right now - and draws as a date range with a dashed connector and an "In progress" tag. It deliberately does not take the "Up next" tag: what is next is the milestone that ends it, and a reader looking for the date they have to act on should find it on that one.
