<p>
  <strong>Livable CRD is a coalition, not an organization.</strong> The groups
  below already work on transit, housing, climate, arts, walking, cycling,
  healthcare access and governance in this region, year round and mostly with
  volunteers. For the municipal election they pooled the one thing none of them
  could do alone: put the same questions to every candidate in thirteen
  municipalities and publish the answers in one place.
</p>

{% include sponsor-notice.html %}

<h2>What partners do</h2>
<p>
  Each partner writes the questions in its own area of focus and grades the
  answers to those questions, using
  <a href="{{ '/faq/#who-grades' | relative_url }}">a published methodology</a>. A
  transit group does not grade the housing answers, and no partner grades the
  whole questionnaire: the grade a candidate carries on a topic comes from the
  people who work on that topic. The
  <a href="{{ '/questionnaire/' | relative_url }}">full question set</a> is
  public, so anyone can see which questions were asked before seeing what
  anyone scored.
</p>
<p>
  Partners are not endorsing anyone by taking part, and neither are we. Listing
  an organization here says it helped build the questionnaire — nothing about
  who it wants you to vote for.
</p>

<h2>Joining</h2>
<p>
  The coalition is open to organizations working on livability in the Capital
  Regional District. If your group wants to add questions in its area of focus,
  or to help with the work of collecting and publishing answers, email
  <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}?subject=Joining%20the%20coalition">{{ site.email }}</a>.
  <a href="{{ '/donate/' | relative_url }}">Donations</a> from individuals pay
  for the site and the outreach; partners contribute time rather than money.
